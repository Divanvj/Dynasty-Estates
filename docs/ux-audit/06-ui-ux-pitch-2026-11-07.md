# Dynasty Estates: what to improve next (UI/UX pitch)

*7 November 2026. A desktop (1280 px) and phone (375 × 812) walkthrough of the running app on localhost:3000 (current `main`), plus a source check and the Impeccable design scanner.*

---

## The short version

Dynasty already gets the basics right. Nothing scrolls sideways on a phone, every image has alt text, every button has a label, and text is never smaller than 12 px. The audit work from S1 to S8 shows.

What holds it back now is not polish. It is **trust and focus**:

1. **Some things look real but aren't.** A buyer who notices one of them stops trusting the rest.
2. **The site feels like five apps.** Each page has its own header, logo and vocabulary.
3. **The important thing is not always the loudest thing.** On a listing that means the home, the price and the agent. On search it means the results.
4. **On phones, the listing page hides its own name and price.**

Fixing those four makes Dynasty feel like a serious, trustworthy property site. Everything else is a refinement.

---

## 1. Stop showing things that aren't real *(highest priority)*

A property site runs on trust. These items look like working features or real data:

| What a visitor sees | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| **"Floor Plan"** tab with "Download PDF Blueprint" and "verified by practitioner" | When a listing has no floor plan, a stock Unsplash photo of a *different house's exterior*. The "blueprint" download opens that photo. |
| **"Continue with Apple"** on sign-in | The button does nothing. Its icon is also missing, so it shows an empty box. |
| **"South African Metros at a Glance"** on the home page (+7.8%, +6.4%…) | Figures typed into the code, with no "sample" label. The Insights page is labelled as sample data; this panel isn't. |
| **"Our Pretoria East Shortlist · 2 collaborators · 4 votes cast"** on the home page | Typed into the code. It is the same for every visitor. |

**What we'd change:** hide the Floor Plan tab when there's no plan. Remove the Apple button until Apple sign-in exists. Either label the metro panel "sample" or remove it. Replace the fake shortlists with a real "Start a collection" prompt.

---

## 2. Make it feel like one site

Each section has its own header today:

- **Home:** Buy · Rent · Sell your home · Tools · AI Search · Profile
- **Search:** Home · Collections · Compare · Saved Homes · Profile
- **Listing:** a dark "D" tile, then Search Catalogue · Print · Share · Save · Collection
- **Map:** "Geospatial Intelligence · Dynasty Live Map & Street View"
- **Calculators:** "Property Finance · South African Financial Calculators"

The listing page, the footer and the sign-in page also draw their own "D" square instead of using the real logo.

**What we'd change:** one header on every page, with the same menu and the real logo. Page-specific buttons (Share, Save, Print) move into the page itself, next to the listing title. Visitors learn the navigation once.

---

## 3. Put the important thing first

### Listing page (the page that earns enquiries)
- **Desktop:** the agent card (WhatsApp, Call, Schedule a viewing) sits *below* a long cost calculator. The calculator's "R 33,022 / month" is bigger than the asking price.
  → Move the agent card to the top of the right column. The calculator stays, as an expandable section below it.
- **Desktop:** the agent card has four buttons of similar weight (WhatsApp, Call, Schedule Private Viewing, Message Practitioner).
  → Make **one** main action ("Book a viewing") and show the others as smaller options.
- **Desktop:** the price wraps, leaving "R" alone on its own line.
  → Keep the price on one line.

### Search page
- **Desktop:** For Sale / To Rent appears **twice** in the same panel.
- **Phone:** the first result starts about two-thirds of the way down the screen. Above it sit a toggle, a view switch, a sort menu, "Reset All", the search box and filter chips. There is also an empty grey bar at the top, and no "x results" count.
  → One For Sale / To Rent switch. On phones, put sort, view and filters behind one "Filters (2)" button and show "24 homes" above the first card.
- **Both:** when nothing matches, the message says "No properties matched your criteria" but doesn't say *which* filter caused it.
  → Name the filter ("No 3-bed homes under R2m in Rosebank") and offer a one-tap fix ("Remove price limit").

### Home page
- *(The rotating hero headline is intentional and stays as is.)*
- **Both:** the home page lists the whole catalogue as long cards with full paragraphs, so the page is about 6,500 px tall.
  → Show 6 to 8 featured homes and a "See all homes for sale" button.

## 4. Fix the phone experience

- **The listing name and price disappear behind the photo.** On a phone, the photo is layered on top of the title, the price and the For Sale badge. The page opens on a photo with no name. *(Checked in the browser: the image is the top element where the title should be.)* This is a bug, and the most important phone fix.
- **Prices on photos can't be read on bright pictures.** White "R 3,650,000" over a white building is invisible on the home and search cards.
  → Add a soft dark fade behind the price, or move the price below the photo.
- **Too much fixed chrome on a listing.** The listing has a top bar, a row of icon buttons, a sticky "Asking price / WhatsApp / Viewing" bar and the bottom tab bar. Together they take roughly a third of the screen.
  → Merge the icon row into the top bar. Keep the sticky contact bar, which works well.
- **The bottom tab bar** includes **Insights**, a sample-data page, but has no way to Sell or reach your account.
  → Swap Insights for Account (or Sell).
- **Small fixes:** the search placeholder is cut off ("Search city or subu…"), the spotlight address is cut off, and the map's area chips are cut off ("Pre…").

---

## 5. Speak like a person, not a brochure

Visitors meet internal or salesy words where plain ones would do:

| Today | Plainer |
|---|---|
| View Property Experience → | View home |
| Curated Luxury Homes & Estates | Homes for sale |
| Estate Portfolio · Asset class | Property type |
| Mandate Property Practitioner | Listing agent |
| Geospatial Intelligence | Map search |
| Benchmark Prime · HOC | Prime rate · Home insurance |
| "Role-based access" box on sign-in | *(remove; visitors don't need it)* |

The filter panel also shows a long A–Z strip of every suburb (Alberton, Amanzimtoti, Ballito…). Typing into the search box already does this job, so the strip can go.

---

## 6. Smaller polish *(when time allows)*

- The map panel stays empty for several seconds while loading, with no "loading map…" message.
- Two bouncy animations (the theme toggle and the 3D tour) feel cheaper than the rest of the site. Smooth easing fits the premium feel.
- Many sections open with a tiny spaced-out label ("PROPERTIES FOR SALE", "MARKET INTELLIGENCE"). One or two are elegant; on every section they become noise.
- Each listing card has "Add to Compare →" and "View Property Experience →". The whole card should be clickable, with Compare as a small icon.

---

## What's working (keep it)

- **Phone basics are solid:** no sideways scrolling, a bottom tab bar, and a sticky contact bar on listings.
- **Accessibility groundwork:** labelled buttons, alt text on every image, a readable minimum text size, and an Esc-to-close filter panel with "Clear all".
- **Honest labelling has started:** Insights says "sample data", the footer says "Beta — listings shown are sample data", and past on-show dates are hidden.
- **The calculator itself is genuinely useful** for South African buyers (transfer duty, bond, levies). It just needs to sit lower on the listing page.
- **The visual base is calm and premium.** The teal palette, rounded panels and photo-led spotlight fit the brand. No rebrand is needed.

---

## Suggested order

| Priority | Theme | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Remove fake or dead features (§1) | Cheapest fixes, biggest trust gain, no design decisions needed |
| **2** | Phone listing bug and price legibility (§4, first two items) | Real bugs that every phone visitor sees |
| **3** | One header everywhere (§2) | Everything else builds on it |
| **4** | Listing and search priorities (§3) | Where enquiries are won or lost |
| **5** | Plain language (§5) | Quick, and it changes how the whole site feels |
| **6** | Polish (§6) | Last |

---

## Not covered by this pass

- **Signed-in areas:** the seller portal, agent dashboard and admin. I wasn't signed in.
- **Dark mode.**
- **Keyboard-only and screen-reader walkthroughs.**
- **A real phone:** the phone view was an emulated screen, not a device with touch.
- **Speed:** load times weren't measured. The earlier audit's performance step (S15) is still open.
- **Steps S9 to S15** from the earlier plan (`04-implementation-plan.md`) are not done. Several of the items above overlap with them (search states, cards, listing page, seller portal), and the implementation plan should merge the two lists.
